WIND ISLE / 风屿 — exploration prototype 0.1.0

Original procedural terrain, character, props, vegetation, animation, shaders,
interface and synthesized audio created for this prototype.
No Nintendo characters, maps, music, textures or other game assets are used.

Rapier JavaScript compatibility package 0.12.0 — Apache License 2.0.
Physics engine by Dimforge and contributors, https://rapier.rs/.
See licenses/RAPIER-APACHE-2.0.txt.

The rigid-body engine handles collision and dynamics. Buoyancy, wetness,
combustion and thermal lift are simplified game models, not engineering
or scientific simulation. The terrain, scenery and character are a prototype.
